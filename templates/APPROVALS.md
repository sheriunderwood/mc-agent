# Approvals

Things only an owner can decide. The agent adds items here with
`request_approval`. When an owner is online, it lets them know items are
waiting, and records their decision from their IM with `resolve_approval`.
You can also decide an item here by hand: change `[ ]` to `[x]` (approved) or
`[-]` (declined) and add a note.

Offers in Second Life can expire. If one has expired, the agent says so instead
of acting on it.
