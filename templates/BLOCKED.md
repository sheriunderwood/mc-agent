# Blocked

People the avatar never talks to: no replies, greetings, notes or questions.
Their messages are ignored, though anything the owner should know is still
reported.

The agent adds people here with `block_person` when they're abusive, and
queues an approval so the owner can review it. **Only an owner unblocks**, by
deleting the row. Owners can't be blocked.
